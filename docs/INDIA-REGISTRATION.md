## Software copyright

Copyright in protectable original work arises automatically in India; registration is optional and provides evidence relating to ownership. It does not protect the abstract idea of a multi-connection downloader, and the FetchVolt name by itself is not ordinarily protected by copyright. Copyright ownership, trademark registration and software licensing are separate matters.

Use IP India's current copyright e-filing services and Form XIV, selecting the Software category. Prepare accurate author/applicant details, the work's title, authorship and ownership history, publication status/dates, Statement of Particulars and Further Particulars, signature and any required permissions/NOCs. Do not invent first-publication details: confirm what was publicly distributed and when from release records.

Current official software filing guidance asks for a PDF under 10 MB containing at least the first 10 and last 10 pages of source code, or the entire code if under 20 pages, without blocked-out or redacted portions. Follow the current portal's work-upload and acknowledgement instructions. Some older official FAQs still describe source and object code; resolve any conflict with the current filing portal or the office before submitting. Pay the applicable official fee and retain the acknowledgement/diary number. Do not claim registration until it is granted.

Before claiming exclusive authorship, document your human-authored contributions, AI-assisted development, third-party components and permissions. An Indian IP professional should assess the actual ownership/protectability claim, including the logo. Do not claim ownership of dependency authors' work. This project does not create a filing-ready source-code PDF or submit a declaration automatically.

## FetchVolt name and logo trademarks

Start with a registry search for identical and confusingly similar marks, including phonetic variants, covering relevant goods/services. The earlier general web name search is not official trademark clearance. Decide whether to seek a word mark for FetchVolt, a logo mark, or both; they are distinct filings/protection choices.

Class 9 is a likely starting class for downloadable Windows software; this is an assessment from the product and WIPO's classification, not a completed classification/clearance opinion. Include other classes only if your actual goods/services require them. Prepare the applicant's accurate details, representation of the mark, goods/services description and truthful proposed-use or prior-use particulars, with an affidavit/authorization if applicable. File TM-A through the official trademark e-filing gateway.

The official schedule lists electronic TM-A filing at INR 4,500 per mark per class for an Individual/Startup/Small Enterprise, and INR 9,000 for other applicants. Eligibility, extra classes, separate marks and professional fees affect the total. Check the portal at payment time. Examination, possible objections and the opposition process follow filing; filing is not registration. Do not use the registered-mark symbol before registration.
